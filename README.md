## 📝 Key Course Modules

* **Ethical Frameworks:** Ethical Analysis, Virtue Ethics, and Codes of Ethics (including SDAIA & DASCA).
* **Privacy & Security:** Anonymity, Information Disclosure, and Data Security.
* **Fairness & IP:** Mitigating Bias in AI systems and understanding Intellectual Property.
* **Cybersecurity:** Focused ethics for cybersecurity practices (Week 16).
